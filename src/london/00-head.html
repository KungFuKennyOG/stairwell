The Bag

Degsy's got a bag on the boot of his motor that needs to get to Arch 9, under the railway bridge. Two coppers are between you and it. Don't run. Don't let them see it.

Phone: thumbstick to walk, drag to look, RUN, GRAB and JUMP (landscape works best). Desktop: mouse to look, WASD to walk, Shift to run, E or click to grab, Space to vault or climb. Headset: left stick to walk (click it to run), right stick to turn, squeeze with a hand on the bag to grab it, A or X to vault or climb. Look at someone up close to meet them.
