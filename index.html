Puerto Seco · level 3 · the stash house

Stairwell

Colonia Tierra Blanca, 02:40. The stash house is at the far east end of the neighbourhood; you start at the west end. Twenty men between you and it. Get there along the dry canal, down the south street past the plaza and the soccer court, or through the vecindad and the mechanic's yard. Off the route: the junkyard, the cantina and its back room, the plaza and the church, each with a weapon worth taking. Three halcones (lookouts) watch the streets: if one sees you and is still standing two seconds later, he radios it in and the whole crew comes. The generator in the stash house yard runs the compound: shoot it and everyone near comes, or flip the red switch on it by hand (poke it in VR, G at a desk) and only the nearest two come to look. The crew's ledger is on the count table. The shaft in the back room goes down to the tunnel. The rack and spawn board are where you start; the level button on the board cycles stash house, tunnel, tower floor.

In VR you start with your fists. Grip grabs from the rack, the floor or a body, grip again drops or throws. Trigger fires; push a suppressor (SUP9 for the 9mm and MP5, SUP12 for the shotgun) onto the muzzle to put it on, grip it with your empty hand to take it off; point a gun at the floor for half a second to reload it (A/X works too), B/Y kicks. Punch and cut by actually swinging. Poke the board buttons to spawn people. Left stick moves, click it for bullet time; right stick turns, click it for sharpness. Goggles: touch the top of your head with an empty hand and squeeze grip to cycle up, night vision, thermal. Your frame rate is on your left wrist.
