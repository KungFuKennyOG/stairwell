Mic: talk and the men close by hear a voice; shout and everyone knows where you are, but the people who can see you do what they're told (hands up, get down, stop). Whispering is silent. The bar under the FPS on your left wrist shows your level. Flat screen: H shouts.

You leave the lobby with Novak's pistol; the men upstairs have the rest (a shotgun on 02, an MP5 on 03). Grip grabs a weapon off a table, the floor or a body, or grabs a man (collar, wrist, neck from behind); grip again drops or throws. Trigger fires; point a gun at the floor for half a second to reload it (A/X works too), B/Y kicks. Punch and cut by actually swinging. Step onto the east landing to go up a floor (it fades straight there); the game saves on the stairs, and dying puts you back at the start of the floor. Left stick moves, click it for bullet time; right stick turns, click it for sharpness. Your frame rate is on your left wrist.
